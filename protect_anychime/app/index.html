Setup & compatibility

Add new speakers to Home Assistant first, then refresh this list. The list includes third-party entities that advertise media playback. Native UniFi devices, integrations known to reject chime audio URLs, and entities without playback capability are hidden. A successful playback request does not guarantee audible output. Actual audio playback depends on what its Home Assistant integration can do. Devices already grouped in Home Assistant may ring together; the bridge keeps those groups unchanged.

Device images are matched using the manufacturer and model reported by Home Assistant. A known related model may be shown when an exact image is unavailable; otherwise a generic icon is used.

Removing a connected virtual chime in Protect deselects its speaker here. Removing a member leaves its chime intact. Removing the last member requires confirmed removal from Protect. Your local Protect account must be configured in the HA app. Back up and restore AnyChime through Home Assistant. Include this app in your backup to keep your speaker selection, chime identities, console connection and ringtones.

Ringtone, volume and repetition are configured in UniFi Protect. Connection details are managed here. Existing speaker selections and virtual device identity are preserved when updating.

Device images use UniFi’s catalogue where available. If your exact model is missing, a similar or older model may be shown. Your actual device details stay unchanged.
